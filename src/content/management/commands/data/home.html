Given the unprecedented data generated at JPL and with NASA, data science has rapidly become a critical capability for JPL’s future. JPL has developed a world class data science team researching and deploying cutting edge capabilities in data science, machine learning, big data, and artificial intelligence spanning science, mission operations, engineering, and other areas. These capabilities are critical to ushering in a data-driven future that will unlock insight and new opportunities to push the frontier of space and earth exploration.

JPL’s critical capability areas include:

  • Machine Learning
  • Scalable data architectures (Data lifecycle)
  • Advanced big data/cyberinfrastructure infrastructures
  • Information architectures (Information models, Ontology)
  • Statistical methodologies
  • Visualization
  • Data preservation and management

JPL has launched many initiatives, pilots, and projects. We invite you to explore our data science universe.
